Our Game

Mythos Block
Greek Mythology Block Puzzle · Free · Android

A relaxing 8×8 block puzzle set in the world of Greek myth. Drop blocks, clear lines, and awaken the gods of Olympus — no timers, no pressure.

  • 11 sagas and 276 stages, from the Trojan War to Primordial Chaos
  • 80 heroes, gods, and monsters to collect, each with original artwork
  • Signature hazards for every saga — petrify, spreading fire, hydra regrowth, and more
  • 5 bonus mini-games included
  • Works offline · 8 languages supported
